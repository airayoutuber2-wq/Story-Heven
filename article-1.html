A Letter I Never Sent

Sometimes the hardest words to say are the ones that matter most.

Every evening, Daniel passed the little bookstore on the corner. And every evening, he saw Emma sitting beside the window, reading beneath the warm yellow light.

They had spoken many times. They had shared small jokes, rainy walks and quiet smiles. But there was one thing Daniel had never said.

One winter evening, he finally wrote it down.

He folded the letter carefully and placed it inside his coat. The next morning, he went to the bookstore.

The chair beside the window was empty.

Emma had moved to another city.

Daniel stood there for a long moment, holding the letter he had never given her.

Years later, he still kept it. Not because he was waiting for her to return, but because the letter reminded him that some feelings remain meaningful even when they are never spoken.

And that was the story of the letter he never sent.
